ANICANTO · CONCEPT ARTWORK

01 / CREATIVE AI

AniCanto ↗

Local AI dubbing for Japanese animation, in Hong Kong Cantonese, English or both. Speech recognition, translation, voices and selectable subtitles come together in one desktop workflow. After downloading the models, processing runs on your computer without a cloud model.

Why it matters to meBringing the sound and personality of Hong Kong Cantonese into a creative hobby I enjoy.

Speech & translationDesktop appLocal LLMPublic beta
Inside the project +

Japanese dialogue → transcription → Cantonese / English script → voice and subtitle tracks. Translations and generated voices need listening and review.

SCRIPT BRIDGE USE WITH FILEMAKER · CONCEPT ARTWORK

02 / DEVELOPER TOOLS

Script Bridge use with FileMaker ↗

Script Bridge is a cross-platform app for Mac and Windows, for use with FileMaker. Draft scripts with optional on-device Qwen AI, or bring in text from another AI tool. Review and validate the draft, then convert it into native FileMaker clipboard steps before you paste.

Why it matters to meSpending more time shaping a useful script and less time copying the same steps by hand.

Smarter home charging ↗

Connecting Intelligent Octopus Go with FoxESS so EV charging doesn’t drain the home battery. A Raspberry Pi server keeps the automation running, with a mobile web dashboard for schedules and live energy data. A standalone web edition is available too.

WHY AI FITS INTO MY WORK / 03

A useful coworker.
A clearer workflow.

For me, AI is most useful as a coworker inside the tools I’m already using—with the context to help and a clear view of what it is doing.

It can help interpret records, prepare a draft or explain the next step. I aim to make the handovers feel seamless while keeping sources, progress and decisions visible. Less chasing and repeated admin means more attention for the work that needs a person.

01Start with the real task. Start with the relevant files and records.

02Make the work transparent. Show sources, uncertainty and proposed changes.

03Keep the person in control. Review before applying consequential actions.
